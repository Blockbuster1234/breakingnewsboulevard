Iran Talks Collapse in Islamabad - US VP Vance: "They Chose Not to Accept Our Terms"

📅 April 12, 2026 | 🌍 World News | ✍️ Breaking News Boulevard

BREAKING: After 21 hours of intensive negotiations in Islamabad, US Vice President Vance announced that Iran has rejected Washington's terms for ending the Hormuz blockade. The war continues.

21 Hours of Diplomacy End in Failure

US Vice President JD Vance led the American delegation in what was described as the most serious diplomatic effort to end the Iran conflict since it began in late February. The talks, hosted by Pakistan in Islamabad, lasted 21 consecutive hours.

"Iran chose not to accept our terms," Vance said in a brief press conference after the negotiations collapsed. "The door remains open, but for now, the path forward is unclear."

Iran's Response: "Forward-Looking Initiatives" Rejected

Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf, leading the Iranian delegation, said Tehran had presented "forward-looking initiatives" during the talks. However, he did not provide details on what those proposals entailed.

Iran's position remains firm: the Hormuz blockade will not be lifted until international sanctions are completely removed and US military forces withdraw from the Persian Gulf region.

Global Impact: Fuel Shortages Loom

The EU airline industry warned today of potential fuel shortages if the Strait of Hormuz remains closed. ACI Europe said airports across the continent are "increasingly concerned" about jet fuel availability.

Oil prices have stabilized around $98/barrel following the news, but analysts warn of another spike if military action resumes.

What's Next?

US President Trump is expected to address the nation this evening. Pentagon officials have not ruled out expanded military operations against Iranian infrastructure.

The Artemis II crew, currently returning to Earth after their historic lunar mission, symbolically landed in Houston today — a reminder that while Earth remains in conflict, humanity's reach extends beyond the stars.
