When Will Results Be Announced for England, Scotland and Wales Elections?

2026-05-08 | Politics

Voters turned out in record numbers for the local government elections, with polls closing precisely at 22:00 BST. Counting stations opened their doors as soon as the last ballot was deposited, and officials began the process of verifying and sorting votes.

According to the Electoral Commission, the initial tranche of results—covering constituencies that reported first—should be available by 02:00 a.m., giving commentators an early indication of party performance. However, the commission cautioned that areas with high volumes of postal ballots may not finish until later in the morning.

"We have robust procedures in place to ensure every vote is counted accurately and swiftly," said Jenny Hughes, a spokesperson for the Electoral Commission. "While we aim to have the majority of results out by dawn, we will not rush the process at the expense of integrity."

"The timing hinges on how many postal votes each region has to process," noted Dr. Liam Patel, politics professor at the University of Edinburgh. "In Scotland, where postal voting is traditionally higher, we could see a delay of several hours compared to England and Wales."

For now, voters and parties alike are watching the live feeds, anticipating the first indications of who will control councils and mayors across the three nations. The final outcome will shape policy debates heading into the next national election cycle.
