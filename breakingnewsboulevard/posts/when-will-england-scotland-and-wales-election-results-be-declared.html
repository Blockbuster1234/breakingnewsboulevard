When Will England, Scotland and Wales Election Results Be Declared?

2026-05-08 | Politics

Polling stations in England, Scotland and Wales closed at 10 p.m. local time on Thursday, triggering the start of the count. According to the UK Electoral Commission, the first tranche of results — primarily from English local councils — should begin appearing after midnight, with the bulk of the declarations expected between 2 a.m. and 5 a.m. on Friday. "We have robust procedures in place to ensure accuracy while working as swiftly as possible," said Commission spokesperson Laura Greene.

The timetables differ slightly for the devolved elections. In Scotland, the Scottish Parliament count includes a significant number of postal ballots that must be opened and verified, which can add an extra hour or two to the process. Wales faces a similar situation with the Senedd election, where returning officers are prioritising the validation of postal votes before moving to in‑person ballots. Dr. Sarah Mitchell, professor of politics at the University of Edinburgh, noted, "While the core counting machinery is the same, the extra verification steps for postal votes mean Scotland and Wales may see their final results slightly later than England."

Logistical challenges have also been highlighted this year, with record‑high turnout reported in several constituencies and a surge in postal voting due to ongoing public health considerations. John Hughes, a senior election officer overseeing the count in Manchester, explained, "We’re processing more ballots than in previous years, and each packet has to be checked for signatures and eligibility before it can be tabulated, which naturally extends the timeline."

Despite the delays, officials are confident that the majority of seats will be called by early Friday morning, with the final declarations likely completed by 8 a.m. BBC political editor Tom Watson advised viewers to stay tuned to live coverage, saying, "Expect a steady stream of updates throughout the night; the picture will become clearer as each constituency reports, and we’ll bring you the decisive moments as they happen."
